## What changed after 2019

The most important dividing line in the recent Hesse-linked data is 2019. That is when Starlink, SpaceX’s satellite internet constellation, began turning into a common skywatching problem. The International Astronomical Union notes that the number of satellites has increased rapidly since 2019, with plans for tens of thousands of new objects in orbit; CENAP-linked reporting in Germany shows the same change from the public-observer side, with Starlink repeatedly producing strings of lights that look unfamiliar to casual viewers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://iauarchive.eso.org/public/themes/satellite-constellations/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: iauarchive.eso.org">[IAU Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">iauarchive.eso.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IAU Archive Satellite Constellations &#124; IAU</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">IAU Archive Satellite Constellations &#124; IAU</span></span></span><span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fr.de/wissen/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-statistik-2022-ungeklaert-starlink-astronomie-himmel-ungeklaert-zr-92045530.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fr.de">[FR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on fr.de.</span></span></span>

This matters because [Starlink sightings]({{'starlink-wave/' | relative_url }}) are not isolated, one-off mistakes. They can arrive in bursts. Newly launched satellites can appear as a line of bright points moving together, especially shortly after launch, before they spread out and become less striking. Frankfurter Rundschau’s account of CENAP’s 2022 statistics said Starlink had been keeping the reporting centre busy since its first launch in 2019 and described the familiar “string of pearls” effect that prompts many people to puzzle over what they have seen.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fr.de/wissen/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-statistik-2022-ungeklaert-starlink-astronomie-himmel-ungeklaert-zr-92045530.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fr.de">[FR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on fr.de.</span></span></span>

The numbers show how quickly one new technology can reshape a UFO dataset. In 2022, CENAP reported more than 729 incoming reports, with Starlink again near the front of the explanations. In 2023, the total rose to 807, but the leading causes shifted: Venus, Jupiter and Sirius together overtook Starlink, even though Starlink still accounted for 176 reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fr.de/wissen/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-statistik-2022-ungeklaert-starlink-astronomie-himmel-ungeklaert-zr-92045530.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fr.de">[FR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on fr.de.</span></span></span><span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.merkur.de/wissen/jupiter-starlink-astronomie-ufo-meldestelle-807-meldungen-himmel-objekte-stern-planet-venus-zr-92756211.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: merkur.de">[Merkur]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">merkur.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">807 Ufo-Meldungen im vergangenen Jahr – Welche Objekte wirklich dahinterstecken</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">807 Ufo-Meldungen im vergangenen Jahr – Welche Objekte wirklich dahinterstecken...</span></span></span>

By 2024 and 2025, the overall totals had moved into a different range. CENAP counted 1,084 reports in 2024, compared with a usual average of 600 to 800 according to Hansjürgen Köhler, the centre’s co-founder and head. In 2025 the total rose again to 1,348, with Hessischer Rundfunk reporting that CENAP said annual reports had risen continuously since 2019.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span><span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/deutsche-ufo-meldestelle-verzeichnet-neuen-rekord-von-sichtungen-v1%2Cufo-meldestelle-104.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Hesse_19bb21_record_sighting_year_e2776d-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Record Years illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Hesse_19bb21_record_sighting_year_e2776d-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Hesse_19bb21_record_sighting_year_e2776d-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Why report totals can rise for ordinary reasons

A record year sounds dramatic, but a UFO report is not the same thing as strong evidence. It is a record of someone encountering something they could not identify at the time. The count can rise because there are more unusual-looking things in the sky, because people are paying more attention, because they know where to report, or because they can now submit photos and videos more easily.

In Hesse’s case, CENAP’s record years are best read through several overlapping causes:

**More visible satellites.** Starlink is the clearest example. In 2020, Köhler told Die Zeit that 60 per cent of that year’s reports were Starlink. That is not a small background effect; it is a major change in the observing environment. When a single satellite system can account for such a large share of reports, a rise in case numbers cannot be treated as a rise in unknown craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zeit.de/wissen/2025-12/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-ausserirdische-hoechststand-sichtungen/seite-3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zeit.de">[DIE ZEIT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zeit.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">DIE ZEITUfo-Meldestelle CENAP: Eine neue Herausforderung: Elon Musk &#124; DIE ZEIT</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DIE ZEITUfo-Meldestelle CENAP: Eine neue Herausforderung: Elon Musk &#124; DIE ZEIT</span></span></span>

**More bright, familiar astronomical objects being noticed.** NASA’s skywatching guidance says Venus is one of the objects most often confused for a UFO, especially when low on the horizon, and that Sirius, Jupiter and Mercury are also often reported as UFOs. CENAP’s 2023 figures fit that pattern closely: Venus appeared in 164 reports, Jupiter in 83 and Sirius in 31.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/skywatching/night-sky-network/identifying-ufos-and-uaps/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Identifying UFOs and UAPs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Identifying UFOs and UAPs</span></span></span><span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.merkur.de/wissen/jupiter-starlink-astronomie-ufo-meldestelle-807-meldungen-himmel-objekte-stern-planet-venus-zr-92756211.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: merkur.de">[Merkur]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">merkur.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">807 Ufo-Meldungen im vergangenen Jahr – Welche Objekte wirklich dahinterstecken</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">807 Ufo-Meldungen im vergangenen Jahr – Welche Objekte wirklich dahinterstecken...</span></span></span>

**More drones and event lighting.** The modern low-altitude sky is no longer just aircraft, stars and satellites. Hessischer Rundfunk reported that 2024 CENAP cases also included LED balloons, foil balloons, private and industrial drones, aircraft, helicopters, event lighting and bright meteors. The same report noted that mysterious light beams in the Rhine-Main area in February 2024 were apparently sky beamers from a disco, not a UFO incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span>

**More phone evidence, but not always better evidence.** A smartphone photograph can help an investigator, but it can also create new false leads. CENAP says every submitted photo is checked, while Köhler has linked the increase in reports partly to the fact that many people now carry phones and take pictures. That means the reporting pipeline has widened: more people can document a puzzling light, but many images still show lens reflections, distant aircraft, blurred birds, insects, balloons or overexposed points of light.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span>

This is why the Hesse-linked record years should be read as “record reports”, not “record mysteries”. The distinction is central. A busier reporting centre can mean better public engagement with sky phenomena, but it can also mean more ordinary stimuli arriving in the same inbox.

## The useful numbers are the explained ones

The most helpful part of CENAP’s recent statistics is not just the headline total. It is the breakdown of causes. In 2023, CENAP recorded 807 reports, far above the normal average of about 300 to 400 reported in Merkur’s account, but the listed explanations were mostly familiar: Starlink, Venus, Jupiter, meteors, aircraft, drones, Sirius, foil balloons, lighting devices, camera lens reflections and bird images.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.merkur.de/wissen/jupiter-starlink-astronomie-ufo-meldestelle-807-meldungen-himmel-objekte-stern-planet-venus-zr-92756211.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: merkur.de">[Merkur]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">merkur.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">807 Ufo-Meldungen im vergangenen Jahr – Welche Objekte wirklich dahinterstecken</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">807 Ufo-Meldungen im vergangenen Jahr – Welche Objekte wirklich dahinterstecken...</span></span></span>

A flap can feel like a wave of mystery from the outside, but inside the data it may be several ordinary waves stacked together: a bright Venus season, repeated Starlink passes, a few meteor events, drone publicity, and a run of widely shared photographs. The public sees “more UFOs”; investigators see more identification work.

The 2024 record works the same way. CENAP’s 1,084 reports were said to be mostly traceable to Starlink satellites, while other cases involved planets, stars, balloons, drones, aircraft, helicopters, event lighting and meteors. It strengthened a more grounded interpretation: modern observers are facing a sky in which natural, technological and optical stimuli overlap.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/wegen-starlink-und-drohnen-rekordzahl-an-ufo-sichtungen-in-deutschland-eingeschickt-v1%2Cufo-sichtungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span>

## How to read record years cautiously

A careful reader should treat Hesse-linked record years as evidence about reporting conditions first and evidence about unexplained phenomena second. That does not mean witnesses are foolish or dishonest. It means the raw count is a social and technical signal before it is a mystery signal.

The first caution is geographical. CENAP is based in southern Hesse, but its totals are not Hesse-only sighting totals. They cover reports from Germany, Austria and Switzerland, with some from other countries. The Hesse connection is institutional: the state hosts a nationally visible reporting and triage centre. That makes Hesse important in German UFO history, but it does not turn every CENAP report into a Hessian incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hessenschau.de/panorama/deutsche-ufo-meldestelle-verzeichnet-neuen-rekord-von-sichtungen-v1%2Cufo-meldestelle-104.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hessenschau.de">[hessenschau.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hessenschau.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on hessenschau.de.</span></span></span>

The second caution is definitional. A UFO report begins as “unidentified to the observer”, not “unidentified after investigation”. NASA’s practical guidance makes this point in everyday terms: a good identification needs date, time, location, duration, direction, brightness and movement, because those details allow comparison with planets, satellites, meteors, aircraft and other known causes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/skywatching/night-sky-network/identifying-ufos-and-uaps/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Identifying UFOs and UAPs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Identifying UFOs and UAPs</span></span></span>

The third caution is statistical. Report counts are sensitive to attention. A 2024 study using US National UFO Reporting Center data found strong seasonal variation, with more reports in good-weather summer months, and described large swings over time that suggest changing public attention can strongly affect sighting totals. That study is not about Hesse, but it supports a useful general lesson for interpreting Hesse-linked records: counts measure people’s encounters with the sky and their willingness to report them, not just the objective number of strange aerial events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-024-04182-z" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nature.com">[Nature]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nature.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on nature.com.</span></span></span>

The fourth caution is evidential. A record year can produce more solved cases without producing better unsolved cases. In fact, the more a reporting centre receives repeatable Starlink, Venus, drone and balloon cases, the more the archive may grow while the genuinely hard residue remains small, poorly documented or dependent on missing details.

## What the record years really mean for Hesse

For Hesse’s UFO history, the record years matter because they show the state’s modern role as an identification centre. The important story is not a single spectacular case over Frankfurt, Wiesbaden, Kassel or the Odenwald. It is the way a Hesse-based civil reporting hub sits between public curiosity and a changing sky.

That gives the Hesse branch of German UFO history a distinctive value. It shows how a “UFO wave” can be produced by a mix of sincere witnesses, new satellite infrastructure, bright planets, drones, event lighting, camera artefacts and media attention. It also shows why local and regional context matters: the same report total can be misread as a mystery surge or understood as evidence of improved reporting and more identifiable sky traffic.

The strongest conclusion is deliberately modest. Hesse-linked report totals have risen sharply since 2019, and 2024 and 2025 were record years for CENAP. But the numbers do not point to stronger alien evidence. They point to a busier sky, a wider reporting pipeline and a public increasingly likely to photograph and submit what it cannot immediately explain.
