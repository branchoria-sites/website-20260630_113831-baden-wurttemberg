## Introduction

Mannheim’s place in Baden-Württemberg’s UFO history is not built around a single famous mystery in the sky. Its importance is more practical and more unusual: the city helped produce Germany’s best-known civilian UFO reporting and debunking tradition. From the 1970s, Hansjürgen Köhler and Werner Walter turned a teenage interest in strange aerial reports into CENAP, a private reporting centre that became strongly associated with sceptical, identification-led UFO work. Its guiding question was not “could this be alien?” but “what known object or event could have produced this report?”<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hjkc.de/25.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hjkc.de">[CENAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hjkc.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">CENAPRaumfahrt+Astronomie-Blog von CENAP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Über CENAP...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Mannheim" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
That approach matters for Baden-Württemberg because it shaped how many German UFO reports were received, filtered, explained and reported to the public. The Mannheim tradition treated witnesses as people who had often seen something real but misread it under unfamiliar viewing conditions: Venus, Sirius, satellites, aircraft, balloons, drones, reflections, meteors or space debris. It did not make the state’s UFO history less interesting. It changed the centre of interest from spectacular claims to the harder public task of separating unresolved reports from poorly understood but ordinary sky phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell/baden-wuerttemberg/rekord-ufo-meldungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sichtungen in Deutschland auf Rekord-Niveau - SWR Aktuell...</span></span></span>

## Why Mannheim became a UFO scepticism centre

CENAP’s roots go back to Mannheim in the early 1970s. Köhler’s own account says CENAP grew out of the Private UFO Research Group Mannheim, founded in 1973, before CENAP itself was founded in Mannheim in 1976 by Köhler and Werner Walter. In those first years, the group built an archive, developed international contacts and presented itself as a new critical basis for UFO research in Germany.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hjkc.de/25.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hjkc.de">[CENAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hjkc.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">CENAPRaumfahrt+Astronomie-Blog von CENAP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Über CENAP...</span></span></span>

The origin story is revealing because it was not simply a story of disbelief. Forbes Austria reports that Köhler and Walter’s idea emerged after a visit to an observatory where the telephone was left off the hook because staff were being interrupted by calls about strange sightings. The young investigators saw a gap: ordinary witnesses had no obvious public contact point that could take a report seriously without turning every light in the sky into a flying saucer story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.forbes.at/artikel/der-ufo-enttarner" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: forbes.at">[Forbes]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">forbes.at</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Der Ufo-Enttarner</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Der Ufo-Enttarner - Forbes...</span></span></span>

Mannheim therefore became important less as a “hotspot” of extraordinary objects than as a reporting hub. Walter’s Mannheim flat and phone line became part of German UFO culture. Deutschlandfunk describes Walter in 2003 sitting in the UFO reporting office in Mannheim, and notes that for decades he ran a telephone hotline where sightings could be reported. According to that account, agencies including ESA, air traffic control and police forwarded reports to him, while Walter followed up thousands of cases and repeatedly identified ordinary causes behind dramatic descriptions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschlandfunk.de/zum-todestag-von-werner-walter-deutschlands-bekanntester-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschlandfunk.de">[Deutschlandfunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschlandfunk.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zum Todestag von Werner Walter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zum Todestag von Werner Walter - Deutschlands bekanntester UFO-Skeptiker...</span></span></span>

This is why Mannheim belongs in a Baden-Württemberg UFO history even though CENAP’s present centre of activity is now associated with Lützelbach in Hesse. The institutional habit was formed in Mannheim: take the call, collect basic details, compare the sighting against astronomy, aircraft and space activity, and then explain the result publicly where possible. The later move did not erase the Mannheim tradition; it exported it.<img src="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Mannheim illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## The origins of CENAP in Mannheim

CENAP’s early development came out of the crowded and sometimes belief-driven West German UFO scene of the 1970s. A retrospective from the Society for the Study of the UFO Phenomenon notes that Walter’s interest was triggered by a 1973 sighting of a trapezoid-shaped object, after which he and Köhler founded the Private UFO Research Group Mannheim. In 1976, that group became CENAP.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufo-forschung.de/kurz-notiert/werner-walter-verstorben-aus-jufof-nr-227" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufo-forschung.de">[UFO Forschung]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufo-forschung.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufo-forschung.de.</span></span></span>

The important point is that early CENAP was not born as a government office, a university project or a secret archive. It was a private, volunteer effort by amateur astronomers and investigators who wanted to build a more critical method. Köhler’s CENAP page says the group spent its first three years building a large archive and international contacts before establishing a critical basis for UFO research that, in CENAP’s view, Germany had lacked.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.hjkc.de/25.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hjkc.de">[CENAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hjkc.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">CENAPRaumfahrt+Astronomie-Blog von CENAP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Über CENAP...</span></span></span>

That archive culture became one of CENAP’s defining features. The CENAP Report, published from 1976 to 2007, later became available online through a digitisation project. The archive project described 305 issues and more than 13,800 pages of material, covering German and international UFO claims, famous myths, media controversies and sceptical case analyses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.openpr.de/news/1030143/Deutschlands-groesstes-UFO-Archiv-online.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openpr.de">[openPR.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openpr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">open PR.de Deutschlands größtes UFO Archiv online</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">open PR.de Deutschlands größtes UFO Archiv online</span></span></span>

For readers, the archive matters because it shows that Mannheim’s sceptical tradition was not only reactive. CENAP did not merely answer phone calls; it tried to create a memory bank of recurring mistakes. Once enough reports had been collected, investigators could recognise patterns: a bright planet low on the horizon, a string of satellites after launch, a balloon drifting in wind, a lens reflection in a mobile-phone image, or a theatrical light projected onto clouds.

## Hansjürgen Köhler and Werner Walter’s public role

Köhler and Walter became public figures because they made themselves available at the point where curiosity, anxiety and media demand met. Walter was the better-known media sceptic for many years. Deutschlandfunk called him Germany’s most prominent UFO critic after his death in 2016 and described his long-running Mannheim hotline, his amateur astronomy background and his repeated identification of reports as Venus, rocket stages, weather balloons, paper lanterns and other ordinary causes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschlandfunk.de/zum-todestag-von-werner-walter-deutschlands-bekanntester-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschlandfunk.de">[Deutschlandfunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschlandfunk.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zum Todestag von Werner Walter</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zum Todestag von Werner Walter - Deutschlands bekanntester UFO-Skeptiker...</span></span></span>

## How the Mannheim method changed UFO reporting

The Mannheim-CENAP tradition changed German UFO reporting by treating “UFO” as a temporary label rather than a conclusion. A UFO was simply something not yet identified by the witness. Once the object was identified, it stopped being a UFO in the meaningful investigative sense. That sounds obvious, but it cut against a media habit of turning every unresolved witness description into a mystery story.

The method had several practical effects:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **It lowered the barrier for ordinary witnesses.** People did not need to be believers, experts or conspiracy enthusiasts to ask what they had seen.
* **It shifted attention from stories to conditions.** Time, direction, weather, apparent motion, duration, brightness and photographs mattered more than dramatic wording.
* **It built a library of repeat explanations.** The same causes returned again and again: planets, stars, aircraft, satellites, balloons, lanterns, drones, meteors and camera effects.
* **It separated weak reports from genuinely difficult ones.** A case with missing time, no direction and no image might remain unexplained, but that did not make it strong evidence for anything extraordinary.</div>

This was not only a local habit. Deutschland.de describes CENAP as a volunteer-run hotline with an exceptional reputation, cooperating with observatories and the European Space Agency, and says the clarification rate is very high. It also notes an example in which police officers were puzzled by a flashing object in the western sky until Köhler identified it as Sirius under turbulent atmospheric conditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span></span></span>

That example captures the value of the Mannheim tradition. It does not mock the witnesses. The police officers were genuinely baffled. But the explanation came from knowing how a bright star can appear to flash, shimmer or change colour near the horizon. In UFO history, that kind of case is often more useful than a dramatic unsolved story because it shows how sincere observation can still produce a misleading report.<img src="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Mannheim illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Baden-Württemberg reports through a CENAP lens

Baden-Württemberg remains highly relevant to CENAP’s work. In 2024, SWR reported that CENAP counted more than 1,100 UFO reports from Germany, Austria and Switzerland, with 153 from Baden-Württemberg. In absolute numbers, only North Rhine-Westphalia produced more reports, and regional clusters in Baden-Württemberg included [Lake Constance]({{'lake-constance/' | relative_url }}), the Markgräflerland and especially the [Rhine-Neckar]({{'rhine-neckar/' | relative_url }}) region near the border with Rhineland-Palatinate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell/baden-wuerttemberg/rekord-ufo-meldungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sichtungen in Deutschland auf Rekord-Niveau - SWR Aktuell...</span></span></span>

The same figures also show why sceptical interpretation is needed. Baden-Württemberg’s total looks striking, but SWR noted that per million inhabitants the state sat in the national middle range. A busy reporting state is not automatically a state with more genuinely unexplained phenomena. It may be a state with dense population, active media coverage, good sightlines, more cameras, more satellites overhead, and a reporting culture shaped by decades of CENAP visibility.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell/baden-wuerttemberg/rekord-ufo-meldungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Sichtungen in Deutschland auf Rekord-Niveau - SWR Aktuell...</span></span></span>

Recent Baden-Württemberg examples show the pattern clearly. In August 2025, SWR reported that two bright points in the morning sky produced numerous calls to CENAP, especially from Baden-Württemberg, Hessen and Rhineland-Palatinate. Witnesses described them in colourful ways, including as drone twins or strange headlights, but the cause was a close-looking pairing of Venus and Jupiter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell/baden-wuerttemberg/ungewoehnliche-lichter-am-himmel-ueber-bw-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ungewoehnliche lichter am himmel ueber bw 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ufo-Meldestelle erklärt ungewöhnliche Lichter am Himmel - SWR Aktuell...</span></span></span>

That is exactly the sort of case for which the Mannheim tradition was built. The witnesses did not invent the lights. They saw two conspicuous objects in the sky. The error lay in scale, context and expectation. A striking planetary conjunction can feel mechanical or artificial when seen unexpectedly before dawn, especially if a person does not regularly watch the sky.

## The Starlink era gave old sceptical work a new job

CENAP’s sceptical work has become more relevant, not less, in the satellite age. Since 2019, [Starlink satellites]({{'starlink/' | relative_url }}) have repeatedly produced reports because they can appear as a “string of pearls” shortly after launch or as bright moving lights in orbit. ZDF reported that many 2024 reports were linked to satellites, while other identifications included LED balloons, foil balloons, drones, aircraft, helicopters, event lighting, meteors, lens reflections, insects and birds near cameras.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zdfheute.de/panorama/ufo-sichtungen-rekord-cenap-forschungsgruppe-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zdfheute.de">[ZDFheute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zdfheute.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo sichtungen rekord cenap forschungsgruppe 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo sichtungen rekord cenap forschungsgruppe 100</span></span></span>

The newer reports are not always from casual observers. Deutschland.de notes that German Air Traffic Control contacted Köhler after pilots on a passenger aircraft asked about dancing lights on the horizon; Köhler attributed them to sunlight reflecting from Starlink satellites. That is an important corrective to the idea that only inexperienced witnesses misidentify aerial objects. Even trained observers can be surprised by new visual phenomena when they appear in unfamiliar geometry or lighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span></span></span>

By 2025, the burden had grown further. Stern reported that CENAP counted 1,348 sightings in 2025 from Germany, Austria, Switzerland and a few other countries, the highest figure reported by the centre. Köhler again said there had been no alien spacecraft, and identified common causes including bright planets, Sirius, meteors, Starlink, rocket-stage restarts, fuel releases lit by the Sun, re-entering satellites and space debris, as well as a growing number of drone-related reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.stern.de/panorama/weltgeschehen/himmelsphaenomene--ufo-meldestelle-verzeichnet-rekordzahl-37012656.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: stern.de">[stern.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">stern.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Himmelsphänomene: Ufo-Meldestelle verzeichnet Rekordzahl &#124; STERN.de</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Himmelsphänomene: Ufo-Meldestelle verzeichnet Rekordzahl &#124; STERN.de</span></span></span>

This matters for Mannheim’s legacy because it shows that sceptical UFO work is not just old debunking from the flying-saucer era. The objects change. The method survives. Paper lanterns, skybeams and Venus have been joined by satellite trains, drone videos and mobile-phone artefacts, but the core task remains the same: reconstruct what happened before turning a witness’s uncertainty into a mystery claim.

## What CENAP’s scepticism can and cannot prove

CENAP’s work is strongest when it has enough data to test a report: time, location, direction, duration, weather, photographs, videos, known launches, satellite tracks or flight activity. In those cases, the centre can often move a case from “unidentified” to “identified” quickly. SWR’s 2025 interview with Köhler makes this clear: he asks for the date, time, place and direction because those details allow checks against air traffic, orbiting satellites, rocket parts and astronomical causes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell-radio/ufo-meldestelle-cenap-in-den-meisten-faellen-sind-es-drohnen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo meldestelle cenap in den meisten faellen sind es drohnen 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO-Meldestelle CENAP: &quot;In den meisten Fällen sind es Drohnen&quot;...</span></span></span>

The weakness is equally important. If a report lacks basic information, is reported long after the event, has no reliable image, or consists mainly of a memory under surprise, it may remain unresolved without becoming strong evidence. Deutschland.de summarises CENAP’s position by saying the clarification rate is very high and that unsolved cases are mostly due to insufficient information for a conclusive explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschland.de/en/topic/knowledge/ufos-over-germany-hansjuergen-koehler-and-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs over Germany: An expert provides clarity</span></span></span>

That is the most careful way to read Mannheim’s sceptical tradition. It does not prove that every witness was wrong, nor that every historical report has been solved. It shows that a very large share of UFO reporting can be explained when investigators treat the sky as a busy environment rather than an empty stage. The state-level lesson for Baden-Württemberg is therefore modest but valuable: high report numbers, vivid testimony and local media interest are not the same as strong evidence for extraordinary craft.

CENAP also has a cultural limit. Its blunt scepticism has sometimes irritated UFO believers and rival investigators. That friction is part of the history. A sceptical reporting centre can look like a spoiler to people who value unresolved cases as signs of a deeper mystery. Yet the public value of the Mannheim approach lies precisely there: it protects the difference between curiosity and credulity.

## Why Mannheim still matters in Baden-Württemberg’s UFO history

Mannheim matters because it gave Baden-Württemberg a role in the infrastructure of German UFO interpretation. The state’s UFO history is not only a list of lights over towns, airports, valleys or lakes. It also includes the people and institutions that taught the public how to report and question those lights. CENAP’s Mannheim origins connect local amateur astronomy, press-facing investigation, witness support and sceptical case analysis in one unusually durable tradition.

That tradition has three lasting consequences. First, it created a public contact point for people who had seen something they could not explain. Second, it built archives and habits of comparison, making repeat misidentifications easier to recognise. Third, it gave journalists a readily available sceptical source, which helped keep many UFO stories from drifting straight into alien speculation.

For Baden-Württemberg, this makes Mannheim a quiet but central site in the history of UFO reporting. The city did not become famous because a single unambiguous object hovered over it. It became important because Köhler, Walter and CENAP helped define what responsible UFO work could look like in Germany: open to reports, respectful of witnesses, impatient with poor evidence, and strongly committed to identifying the ordinary before claiming the extraordinary.<img src="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Mannheim illustration 3" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_mannheim_cenap_scept_04203d-Illustration-3-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">

    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id ||'10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label ||'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId ||'').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g,'-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g,'');

    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;

      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });

      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });

      label.textContent = selectedText;

      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });

      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ?'true' :'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ?'true' :'false');
    });

    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }

    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') ==='1') return;

        storageKey:'phoenix-ebay-market',

      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }

      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') ==='true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ?'false' :'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
