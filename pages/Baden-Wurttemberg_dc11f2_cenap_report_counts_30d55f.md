## Why the 2023 and 2024 numbers stand out

The 2023 figure matters because Baden-Württemberg led Germany in CENAP’s state-by-state count. Deutschland.de reported that CENAP received 746 sightings across Germany in 2023, with Baden-Württemberg contributing 144, ahead of Bavaria with 124 and North Rhine-Westphalia with 115. That makes the state prominent in the recent German UFO-reporting map, even before the larger 2024 surge.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.deutschland.de/de/topic/wissen/ufos-ueber-deutschland-hansjuergen-koehler-und-cenap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: deutschland.de">[Deutschland]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">deutschland.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufos über Deutschland: Ein Experte klärt auf</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ufos über Deutschland: Ein Experte klärt aufNovember 29, 2024 — 29 Nov 2024 — Die Meldungen zu Ufos reißen nicht ab: Allein 20...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 29, 2024</span></span></span>

The 2024 figure looks even bigger at first glance. SWR reported that CENAP counted more than 1,100 reports from Germany, Austria and Switzerland in 2024, with 153 from Baden-Württemberg alone. In absolute terms, only North Rhine-Westphalia produced more reports. For a public reader, that sounds like a strong “hotspot” signal — and it is, but only in the limited sense that many reports were filed from the state.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell/baden-wuerttemberg/rekord-ufo-meldungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span></span></span>

The per-capita picture softens the headline. SWR noted that Baden-Württemberg’s 2024 figure worked out at 13.5 sightings per million inhabitants, placing it in the national middle range rather than at the top. Rheinland-Pfalz, by comparison, was reported at 28.4 sightings per million inhabitants, more than double Baden-Württemberg’s rate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell/baden-wuerttemberg/rekord-ufo-meldungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span></span></span>

That distinction is crucial. Absolute totals favour large, populous states with many potential witnesses. Per-capita rates tell a different story about reporting intensity. Baden-Württemberg’s numbers are therefore best read as evidence of a large and responsive reporting base, not as proof that unusual objects visit the state more often than elsewhere.<img src="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_cenap_report_counts_30d55f-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Report Counts illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_cenap_report_counts_30d55f-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_cenap_report_counts_30d55f-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## What report totals can and cannot prove

CENAP’s figures prove that people filed reports. They do not prove that each report described a separate object, a genuinely anomalous event, or anything extraterrestrial. The same bright satellite train, planet pairing or meteor can be seen by many people over a wide area, producing multiple reports from one cause. Conversely, a genuinely unusual sighting might generate only one report if few people saw it or if witnesses did not know where to send it.

## How publicity and easy reporting shape the figures

The recent rise in reports is partly a media and technology story. CENAP’s 2024 record came during a period when international discussion of “UAP” was unusually visible, especially after US hearings and official reports. German media also covered CENAP’s work frequently, making the reporting centre more visible to ordinary witnesses who might once have phoned a local observatory, police station or newspaper instead.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zdfheute.de/panorama/ufo-sichtungen-rekord-cenap-forschungsgruppe-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zdfheute.de">[ZDFheute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zdfheute.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo sichtungen rekord cenap forschungsgruppe 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo sichtungen rekord cenap forschungsgruppe 100</span></span></span>

Smartphones also change the reporting pipeline. More people now carry cameras at night, can send images immediately, and can search for a reporting point within seconds. SWR’s account of the 2024 Baden-Württemberg figures specifically linked the increase in reports to the easy availability of phone photos. That increases the number of cases CENAP can inspect, but it also increases the number of ordinary optical effects entering the archive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://meinka.de/rekordzahl-von-ufo-sichtungen-bei-meldestelle-eingegangen/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: meinka.de">[meinKA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">meinka.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rekordzahl von ufo sichtungen bei meldestelle eingegangen</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">rekordzahl von ufo sichtungen bei meldestelle eingegangen</span></span></span>

This is one reason Baden-Württemberg’s numbers should not be treated as a clean natural dataset. They are not a passive instrument reading from the sky. They are a human reporting record shaped by awareness, access, curiosity, local media, weather, visibility, population size and the presence of widely visible triggers. A year with clear evenings, bright planets and high-profile satellite passes can look like a UFO wave even when the underlying causes are ordinary.

The same effect can create regional clusters. SWR identified 2024 reporting concentrations around [Lake Constance]({{'lake-constance/' | relative_url }}), the Markgräflerland and especially the [Rhine-Neckar]({{'rhine-neckar/' | relative_url }}) area near the border with Rheinland-Pfalz. Those clusters may reflect genuine viewing conditions and population patterns, but they may also reflect local attention, regional press coverage and the Rhine-Neckar area’s long connection with CENAP’s history through Mannheim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.swr.de/swraktuell/baden-wuerttemberg/rekord-ufo-meldungen-100.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: swr.de">[SWR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">swr.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">rekord ufo meldungen 100</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_cenap_report_counts_30d55f-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Report Counts illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_cenap_report_counts_30d55f-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Baden-Wurttemberg_dc11f2_cenap_report_counts_30d55f-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Why Baden-Württemberg still matters

The cautious reading is not the boring reading. Baden-Württemberg matters precisely because it shows how modern UFO history often works: not as a chain of confirmed alien encounters, but as a record of public confusion, local investigation and repeated attempts to match witness accounts with known sky phenomena.

CENAP’s roots make the state especially relevant. The organisation is closely associated with [Mannheim]({{'mannheim/' | relative_url }}), where Köhler and Walter began their UFO work in the 1970s before CENAP became one of Germany’s best-known civilian UFO reporting bodies. This means Baden-Württemberg is not only a reporting source; it is part of the institutional history of German UFO scepticism and case identification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrales_Erforschungsnetz_au%C3%9Fergew%C3%B6hnlicher_Himmelsph%C3%A4nomene" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centrales Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher Himmelsphänomene</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centrales Erforschungsnetz außergewöhnlicher Himmelsphänomene</span></span></span>

The 2023 and 2024 counts also help separate three ideas that are often blurred together:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* **A high number of reports** means many people contacted a reporting body.
* **A high number of unexplained cases** would mean many reports resisted identification after serious checking.
* **Evidence of extraordinary craft** would require much stronger, independently verifiable data than a report count.</div>
